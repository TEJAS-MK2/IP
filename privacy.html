Privacy Policy

IP Tracker is designed as a simple public IP lookup tool. We do not ask you to create an account.

Information processed

When you request a lookup, the IP address may be sent to the third-party lookup provider used by this site so it can return approximate network and location information.

What we do not claim

IP geolocation is approximate and should not be treated as an exact physical location. This site does not attempt to identify individuals.

Third-party services

The service may use ipapi.co for lookup data and OpenStreetMap for map display. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Cookies

The site does not require cookies for its core lookup functionality.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the site or its data providers change.
